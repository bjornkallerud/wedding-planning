Is it enough?

Yes

The bar runs about 6 hours (4:15 to 10). The usual rule is one drink per guest per hour: 140 × 6 = 840. We have about 791 plus the aquavit toast and N/A, and the rule counts every guest as a drinker. Most weddings land closer to 4 or 5 drinks per drinker.

Beer and wine

288 beers and 250 glasses of wine. 50 bottles is in line with the usual one bottle per 2 to 3 guests.

Cocktails

About 253 signature cocktails (135 Oaxacan, 118 Old Fashioned), close to 2 per guest at the reception. That's roughly double the original plan of 4 bottles of each.

Watch: white wine

25 bottles of white. If the bluff goes through its 12 fast on a warm afternoon, dinner has 13 left. Hold a few back for the reception.

Watch: Cointreau

2.5 L covers about 112 Oaxacans at 3/4 oz or all 135 at 1/2 oz. Tell the bartender 1/2 oz, or pick up one more 750 ml.
